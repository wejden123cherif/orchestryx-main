Central Banks Signal Coordinated Rate Pause Through Mid-2025

By D. HarringtonFinance Editor

The governors of six major central banks signalled on Wednesday that they intend to hold benchmark interest rates steady through at least mid-year, citing easing inflationary pressure and fragile global growth prospects.

Markets reacted positively, with equities advancing broadly and government bond yields falling to their lowest levels since March. Analysts cautioned the pause is contingent on inflation data remaining within acceptable ranges.
