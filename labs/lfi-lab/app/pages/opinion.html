The Price of Convenience: Why We Keep Handing Our Data to Strangers

By The Editorial Board

Every week brings fresh evidence that the bargain consumers struck with the technology industry — unlimited convenience in exchange for personal data — was struck without full understanding of its terms.

The latest breach, affecting fourteen million users of a popular budgeting application, follows an almost identical incident six months prior. The pattern is familiar: inadequate security posture, delayed disclosure, a press release promising improvements.

Regulation alone will not solve this. The incentive structures must change.

Until the cost of a breach consistently exceeds the cost of prevention, the industry will continue to treat security as an afterthought.
