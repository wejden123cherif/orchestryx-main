Ceasefire Negotiations Enter Third Week as Diplomatic Pressure Mounts

By R. OkonkwoInternational Desk

Talks entered a third consecutive week on Tuesday, with mediators from four neutral nations working through the night to bridge remaining gaps on territorial guarantees and prisoner exchange protocols.

Senior diplomats described the atmosphere as "cautiously constructive" while cautioning that several core disagreements remain unresolved ahead of Friday's deadline set by the UN Security Council.

Aid organisations have called on both parties to allow immediate humanitarian access to affected regions, where an estimated 340,000 civilians remain displaced.
